AITIHASIK

An Ancient India Adventure

Welcome to Aitihasik — an interactive journey into the fascinating world of Ancient India.


Instead of simply reading about kings, kingdoms, battles, art and culture, players discover history by moving, answering, solving and playing.

🎓 Our Goal

Aitihasik is designed especially for children aged 7–12. The goal is to make social and historical knowledge more engaging for children who may find conventional classroom learning less interesting. Through a simple board-game experience, players learn about ancient Indian dynasties while developing curiosity, memory, decision-making and teamwork.

⚔️ Your Mission

Travel through the Maurya and Shunga dynasties, discover important historical facts, complete challenges and collect ancient coins. Reach the final gate — but remember: the first player to reach the end does not automatically win. Once both players reach the end, the player with the most coins becomes the champion.
